Recognition sensitivity

How to tune: in "Recent utterances" compare the similarity of your own phrases with that of TV or guests, and put "Recognized" between them. Higher = fewer mix-ups but more "unknown"; lower = the other way round.

Other voices (for accuracy, optional)

Recordings of people who are NOT in your household: guests, TV, radio, podcasts. They are used only if "Compare with other voices" is switched on in the add-on configuration: then a voice is also compared with these recordings, so household members are confused with strangers less often. You need at least 20–30 recordings (it starts working from 10). Easiest way: in "Recent utterances" send TV speech to "other voices". Sensitivity for this mode is set separately and automatically.
